How it plays

american checkers · 8×8 · no flying kings

  • Standard rule: pieces step one diagonal forward; captures jump onto the empty square beyond and are mandatory. Multi-jumps chain from the landing square.
  • Promotion: reach the far row to become a King (crown glyph, gold ring). Kings move and capture one step in both directions — no flying.
  • Fact wager: each of your captures stages a ~900ms mid-air clash; at the "VS" hold a brand-fact card decides it. Correct = capture counts (+1 battle XP). Wrong or 10s timeout = the attacker returns untouched and the turn passes.
  • One-turn waiver: if a failed wager would leave you without a legal input, mandatory capture is waived for your next turn only.
  • AI captures auto-succeed and show a brand fact toast.
  • Win: opponent has no pieces or no legal moves. Draw: 40 half-moves with no capture or promotion.
