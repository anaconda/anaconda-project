How to play

Standard 8×8 American checkers against the Community engine.

  • Move one diagonal step forward onto a dark square. The green side starts at the bottom.
  • Captures are optional. You may move any piece with a legal move. Jumping is a choice, not a requirement — clicking a jump square plays the whole chain as a single turn.
  • Multi-jumps chain in a single turn while further captures remain.
  • Kings are promoted on the far row and move and capture both directions, one step at a time. No flying kings.
  • Win by capturing every enemy piece or leaving them with no legal move. A draw is called after 40 moves without a capture or promotion.
  • The clash. Every jump is a mid-air fight. Before it resolves, an Anaconda fact card appears and you have 10 seconds to answer True or False.
  • Right answer — the capture counts and your piece gains battle experience.
  • Wrong answer or timeout — the clash is lost, the move is undone, and your turn passes.
  • Fact cards only appear on your captures. The Community engine resolves its own captures automatically and shares a fact as a toast.
